Allocation rule

Teal carries the product. Amber carries uncertainty. Red is spent only on a Critical alert or a destructive action. Nothing else may use red — not even an error count.

Contrast

Body text 12.1:1 on panel. Amber 9.4:1. Red 6.9:1. Teal at 11.4:1. All state text passes 4.5:1 at 100% and survives simulated protanopia because shape and label carry the state.

Field inversion

The same 12 semantic tokens invert to a warm paper base (#F4F3EE) for sunlight. Hues deepen rather than brighten: teal 06645E, amber 7A4A00, red 8F1116.

07

Motion only to explain a change of state

120 / 180 / 240 ms · ease-out
FEEDBACK · 120 ms
Button press, row selection, tab underline. No layout shift.
REVEAL · 180 ms
Explanation tab, filter popover, drawer. Opacity and 4px translate only.
STATE · 240 ms
Risk fill or sparkline endpoint changing on live data. One property at a time.
Never animate a risk score upward. A changing number is not a performance. No pulsing halos, no gradient sweeps, no confetti. prefers-reduced-motion collapses all of it to instant state.

10

Calm, specific, and never certain on the model’s behalf

microcopy pairs
Write

“Effective ballast conductance is 31% above its dry baseline. Rain alone does not explain the slope.”

“This drain is shared by three circuits. Clearing it may resolve all three Watch alerts.”

“The 90% range is 9 to 38 days. Work is already planned on day 2.”

“No fault found is a valid outcome. It still trains the model.”

Do not write

“AI predicts failure with 99% accuracy.”

“Track circuit will fail in 21 days.”

“Action required immediately!!!”

“Technician error caused the failure.”
